# Leaderboard & XP

See how you stack up against other traders on the global leaderboard at **/dashboard/leaderboard**.

## How Rankings Work

Rankings are based on **XP (experience points)**. The more XP you earn, the higher you climb. The **top 3 traders** get a special podium display at the top of the leaderboard.

Your current rank and XP total are always visible at the top of the page so you know exactly where you stand.

## Earning XP

There are several ways to accumulate XP:

- **Referrals** -- earn **+300 XP** for each successful referral

<Callout type="tip">
Invite friends to boost your XP quickly. Each successful referral earns you +300 XP.
</Callout>
