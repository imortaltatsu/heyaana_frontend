# Leaderboard & XP

See how you stack up against other traders on the global leaderboard at **/dashboard/leaderboard**.

## How Rankings Work

Rankings are based on **XP (experience points)**. The more XP you earn, the higher you climb. The **top 3 traders** get a special podium display at the top of the leaderboard.

Your current rank and XP total are always visible at the top of the page so you know exactly where you stand.

## Earning XP

There are several ways to accumulate XP:

- **Referrals** -- earn **+300 XP** for each successful referral
- **Trading volume** -- the more you trade, the more XP you earn
- **Streak bonuses** -- maintain daily trading streaks for bonus XP

## Leaderboard Categories

Filter the leaderboard by category to see who leads in each area:

- **Overall** -- combined XP from all sources
- **Referrals** -- most successful referrals
- **Volume** -- highest trading volume
- **Streaks** -- longest active trading streaks

<Callout type="tip">
Consistency pays off. Daily streaks can add up quickly and push you ahead of traders with higher volume but less regularity.
</Callout>
