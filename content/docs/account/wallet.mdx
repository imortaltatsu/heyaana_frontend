# Wallet Management

When you sign up for HeyAana, a **trading wallet** is created for you automatically. No extra setup needed.

## Your Wallet Address

Your wallet address is visible on your **Profile page**. Click it to copy the full address to your clipboard in one tap.

## Network & Currency

Your wallet operates on the **Polygon** network and holds your trading funds in **USDC**. This keeps transaction fees low and settlements fast.

## Viewing Your Balance

Your wallet balance displays all token holdings along with their **USD values**, giving you a quick snapshot of your available funds.

## MetaMask Integration

<Callout type="info">
For advanced users, you can also connect **MetaMask** for additional wallet management capabilities. This is entirely optional -- your built-in HeyAana wallet handles everything you need for trading.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
Always double-check wallet addresses before sending funds. Blockchain transactions are irreversible.
</Callout>
