# Export Private Key

You can export your wallet's private key from the **Profile page** under **Account & Settings**. This is useful for backup, migration, or accessing your wallet directly on Polymarket.

## How to Export

<Steps>
  <Step number={1} title="Open Account & Settings">
    Go to your Profile page and find the Account & Settings section.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Click Export">
    Select the option to export your private key.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Read the Security Warning">
    A warning will appear explaining the risks. Read it carefully.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Confirm and Reveal">
    Confirm that you understand the risks, then reveal and copy your key.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
**Never share your private key with anyone.** Anyone who has your private key has full control over your wallet and all funds in it. No HeyAana team member will ever ask for your private key.
</Callout>

## Storage Best Practices

- Write it down on paper and store it in a secure location
- Use a password manager with strong encryption
- **Never** store it in plain text on your device, in screenshots, or in cloud notes
