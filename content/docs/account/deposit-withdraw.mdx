# Deposit & Withdraw

Getting funds into and out of your HeyAana wallet is straightforward.

## Deposit

Open the **hamburger menu** and select **Deposit**. HeyAana supports multiple chains with **automatic bridging to Polygon**, so you can deposit from whichever network your funds are on.

## Withdraw

Select **Withdraw** from the menu to transfer **USDC.e** from your Safe trading wallet back to your EOA wallet. You can choose a specific amount or withdraw your full balance.

## Swap USDC

Use the **Swap USDC** feature to convert USDC.e to bridged USDC, which is needed for Polymarket trading.

<Callout type="warning">
The USDC swap feature only works for **EOA wallets**, not Safe wallets. Make sure you're using the right wallet type before swapping.
</Callout>

## Transfer to Safe

The **Transfer to Safe** option moves funds from your EOA wallet to your Safe trading wallet. This is the wallet HeyAana uses to execute your trades.

<FeatureCard title="Quick Reference">
- **Deposit** -- fund your wallet from any supported chain
- **Withdraw** -- move USDC.e from Safe to EOA
- **Swap USDC** -- convert USDC.e to bridged USDC (EOA only)
- **Transfer to Safe** -- move funds from EOA to your trading wallet
</FeatureCard>
