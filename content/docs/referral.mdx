# Referral Program

Invite friends to HeyAana and you both get rewarded.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step number={1} title="Generate a Code">
    Go to **/dashboard/referral** and generate a single-use referral code.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Share It">
    Copy the code to your clipboard or use your device's native share menu.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Get Rewarded">
    When your friend signs up and claims the code, you both earn XP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rewards

- **You** get **+300 XP** for each successful claim
- **Your friend** gets **+200 XP** upon signing up

## Tracking Your Referrals

Your referral page shows all codes you've generated and their claim status, so you always know which invites have been used. You can also view the **top referrers leaderboard** to see how you compare.
