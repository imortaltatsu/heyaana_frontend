# Your First Trade

Prediction markets let you trade on the outcome of real-world events. Here is how to place your first trade.

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Browse markets">
Head to the [Markets](/dashboard/markets) page and find a market that interests you.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Understand pricing">
Prices are shown in cents from 0 to 100, representing the market's implied probability. A "Yes" price of 65 means the market thinks there is a 65% chance the event happens.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Pick a side">
Choose **Yes** or **No** based on what you believe will happen.
</Step>

<Step number={4} title="Enter your amount">
Type in the dollar amount you want to trade.
</Step>

<Step number={5} title="Confirm and buy">
Click **Buy**. If this is your first trade, HeyAnna will automatically handle the one-time Polymarket approval for you.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Use keyboard shortcuts for faster trading: press **B** to buy and **S** to sell.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
HeyAnna is currently in beta. Start with small amounts while you get familiar with the platform.
</Callout>
