# Deposit Funds

Before you can trade, you need funds in your trading wallet on Polygon.

## How to deposit

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Open the Deposit modal">
Click **Deposit** from the sidebar menu. The deposit modal will appear with your wallet address and supported options.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Choose your chain">
HeyAnna supports deposits from multiple chains. Pick the network you are sending from and the system handles bridging to Polygon automatically.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Send funds">
Transfer the desired amount. Once the transaction confirms and bridging completes, your balance will update in the app.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
Double-check that you are sending a supported asset before initiating a transfer. USDC on Polygon is the primary trading currency on Polymarket.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Bridging typically takes a few minutes depending on the source chain. You will see your updated balance as soon as the process finishes.
</Callout>

## Next step

With funds in your wallet, you are ready to [place your first trade](/docs/getting-started/first-trade).
