# Connect & Sign Up

HeyAnna uses Telegram for authentication. No email or password needed -- just your Telegram account.

## How to get started

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Open HeyAnna">
Visit the app and click **Start Trading**, or open the Telegram mini app directly.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Connect via Telegram">
Sign in through the Telegram widget or launch the bot at [@heyanna_ai_bot](https://t.me/heyanna_ai_bot).
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Your wallet is ready">
A trading wallet is automatically created for you on Polygon. No seed phrase to save, no browser extension required.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Your wallet is fully managed -- you do not need to worry about private keys or seed phrases. Just connect and start trading.
</Callout>

## Optional: Connect MetaMask

If you want additional wallet features, you can also link a MetaMask wallet from your profile settings. This is entirely optional and not required to trade.

## Next step

Once you are connected, [deposit funds](/docs/getting-started/deposit) to start trading.
