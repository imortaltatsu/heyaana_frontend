# AI Market Analysis

HeyAana offers on-demand AI analysis for any prediction market, powered by ElsaX402. Get a detailed breakdown of risk, probability, and news context to inform your trades.

## How to use it

1. Open any market detail page
2. Click the **Analyze** button
3. Pay $0.10 USDC (on Base chain) to generate the analysis
4. Review the results directly on the page

<Callout type="warning">
  AI analysis requires a USDC balance on Base chain. Make sure your wallet is funded before requesting an analysis.
</Callout>

## What you get

Each analysis includes:

- **Risk score** -- a 0 to 100 rating of market risk
- **Prediction with probability** -- the AI's projected outcome and confidence level
- **News summary** -- relevant recent news that may impact the market
- **Market view** -- an overall assessment combining all factors

## Payment

Analysis uses the **x402 protocol** for payment. Each request costs **$0.10 USDC** on Base chain. Payment is processed automatically when you click Analyze -- no extra steps required.

<Callout type="tip">
  Use AI analysis on markets where you are unsure about the direction. The risk score and news summary can surface information you might have missed.
</Callout>
