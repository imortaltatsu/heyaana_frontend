# Configuring Signals

All auto-trading configuration lives on the **Auto Trade** page (`/dashboard/auto-trade`).

## Enabling signals

<Steps>
  <Step number={1} title="Navigate to Auto Trade">
    Go to `/dashboard/auto-trade` from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Toggle signal timeframes">
    Enable or disable each timeframe independently -- 5-minute and 15-minute signals each have their own toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Set share size">
    Choose how many shares to buy per signal, from 5 to 1,000 shares. Set this per timeframe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Active signals show a **live status indicator** so you always know what is running.

<Callout type="tip">
  Start with a small share size (5-10) while you learn how the signals perform. You can increase later.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  A **3% fee** is charged on every auto trade execution. The fee is deducted automatically from each trade.
</Callout>

## Execution history

The Auto Trade page includes a full history of every signal execution. Each entry shows:

- **Market title** -- which market the trade was placed in
- **Asset** -- the specific asset traded
- **Direction** -- UP or DOWN
- **Side** -- BUY or SELL
- **Amount** -- number of shares
- **Status** -- whether the trade succeeded or failed
- **Signal time** -- when the signal was generated
- **Execution time** -- when the trade actually executed
- **Transaction hash** -- on-chain proof of the trade

<Callout type="info">
  Use the execution history to evaluate signal quality over time and adjust your configuration accordingly.
</Callout>
