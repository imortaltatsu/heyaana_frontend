# Signal-Based Trading

HeyAana's auto-trading system monitors prediction markets for actionable signals and can automatically execute trades on your behalf.

## How it works

The system continuously scans prediction markets looking for:

- **Unusual activity** -- sudden spikes in volume or interest
- **Liquidity shifts** -- meaningful changes in market depth
- **Price movements** -- significant price action that signals opportunity

When the system detects a signal, it generates a trade recommendation. If you have auto-trading enabled, the trade executes automatically.

## Supported timeframes

<FeatureCard title="5-Minute Signals">
  Short-term signals based on rapid market movements. Best for capturing quick opportunities.
</FeatureCard>

<FeatureCard title="15-Minute Signals">
  Medium-term signals that filter out more noise. Tends to produce fewer but potentially more reliable signals.
</FeatureCard>

You can enable or disable each timeframe independently, so you can run both or just one.

<Callout type="info">
  Signal-based trading is fully automated once enabled. Trades execute without any manual confirmation.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  A **3% fee** is charged on every auto trade execution. This fee is deducted automatically from each trade.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Auto-trading involves risk. Start with small share sizes to understand how the signals perform before scaling up.
</Callout>
