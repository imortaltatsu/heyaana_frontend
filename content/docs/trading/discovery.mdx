# Market Discovery

HeyAnna makes it easy to find markets worth trading. Head to the [Markets](/dashboard/markets) page to start exploring.

## Search and filter

- **Search** -- Find any market by typing keywords into the search bar
- **Categories** -- Filter by Trending, Politics, Sports, Crypto, and more
- **Advanced filters** -- Narrow results by volume, liquidity, or expiry date
- **Sort options** -- Order markets by relevance, volume, or recency

## View modes

Switch between two layouts depending on your preference:

- **Card view** -- Visual cards with key market info at a glance
- **Table view** -- A compact, data-dense list for scanning many markets quickly

## Kalshi markets

In addition to Polymarket, you can browse Kalshi markets at [Kalshi Markets](/dashboard/kalshi-markets) via dFlow integration, giving you access to an even wider range of events.

## Watchlist

Found a market you want to keep an eye on? Add it to your **Watchlist** so you can track it without searching again later.

<Callout type="tip">
Use category filters to quickly zero in on the markets you care about. Combine them with sort options to surface the highest-volume opportunities first.
</Callout>
