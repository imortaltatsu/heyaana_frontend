# Market Orders

Market orders fill immediately at the best available price on Polymarket's Central Limit Order Book (CLOB).

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step number={1} title="Select your side">
Choose **Yes** or **No** on any market.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Enter your amount">
Type in the dollar amount you want to spend.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Confirm the trade">
Review the price (shown as a probability in cents) and click **Buy** or **Sell** to execute.
</Step>
</Steps>

The order is sent directly to Polymarket's order book and fills at the current market price. You will see your new position on your profile page right away.

<Callout type="tip">
If you do not have the right token for the trade, HeyAnna can handle the swap automatically using the auto-prepare option -- no manual conversion needed.
</Callout>

<FeatureCard title="Instant execution">
Market orders are the fastest way to enter or exit a position. Use them when you want to trade immediately without waiting for a specific price.
</FeatureCard>
