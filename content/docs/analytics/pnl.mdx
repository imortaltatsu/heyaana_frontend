# PnL Tracking

HeyAana gives you a clear picture of how your trades are performing, so you always know where you stand.

## Profile Overview

Your **Profile page** displays your total PnL front and center. Profits show in green, losses in red -- no guessing required. Each individual position card shows both the **dollar PnL** and **percentage PnL** so you can quickly scan your portfolio.

## User Analytics Breakdown

Head to your **User Analytics** page for a deeper look:

- **Bar chart** showing PnL for your top 15 positions, ranked by performance
- **Recent trades table** listing your last 30 trades with per-trade PnL
- Filter and sort to find exactly what you need

<Callout type="tip">
Use the recent trades table to spot patterns in your winning and losing trades.
</Callout>

## Share Your Performance

Proud of your results? **PnL share cards** let you generate a visual snapshot of your performance and share it directly to social media. Great for showing off a big win or building your trading reputation.

<Callout type="info">
PnL calculations update in real time as market prices change.
</Callout>
