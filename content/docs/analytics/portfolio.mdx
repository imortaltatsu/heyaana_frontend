# Portfolio Overview

HeyAana gives you visibility into your portfolio across multiple pages, from a quick glance to deep analysis.

## Dashboard home

Your dashboard home page shows summary widgets at a glance:

- **Portfolio value** -- your current total value
- **Recent trades** -- your latest activity
- **Top market** -- the market where you have the most exposure

## Deep analysis

For a full breakdown, head to **User Analytics** (`/dashboard/user-analytics`). This page provides:

- **Portfolio value** -- total value with historical trend
- **Open positions** -- all your currently active trades
- **Total PnL** -- your overall profit and loss
- **Win rate** -- percentage of trades closed in profit

## Charts and visualizations

<FeatureCard title="Exposure by Event">
  A donut chart showing how your portfolio is distributed across different events. Helps you spot concentration risk.
</FeatureCard>

<FeatureCard title="PnL by Position">
  A bar chart breaking down profit and loss for each individual position. Quickly see your winners and losers.
</FeatureCard>

## Position breakdown

The analytics page includes a detailed **position grid** showing every trade with full context: market, direction, entry price, current value, and PnL.

<Callout type="tip">
  Review your analytics weekly to identify patterns in your trading. Look for which market types and strategies are working best for you.
</Callout>
