# CSV Export

Need your trade data outside of HeyAana? You can download your complete trade history as a CSV file in just a few clicks.

## How to Export

<Steps>
  <Step number={1} title="Go to User Analytics">
    Open your User Analytics page from your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Find the Activity Feed">
    Scroll to the activity feed section where your trades are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Click Export">
    Hit the export button to download your CSV file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's Included

Your export contains all the details for every trade:

- **Market** name and category
- **Side** (buy or sell)
- **Size** and **price**
- **PnL** for each trade
- **Timestamps** for when each trade executed
- **Transaction hashes** for on-chain verification

<Callout type="tip">
This export is especially useful for tax reporting, plugging into spreadsheets, or doing your own analysis with external tools.
</Callout>
