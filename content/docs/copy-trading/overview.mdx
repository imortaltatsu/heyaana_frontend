# How Copy Trading Works

Copy trading lets you follow profitable traders and automatically replicate their positions. When a trader you follow makes a trade, the same trade executes in your account -- no manual work required.

## What happens when you copy trade

<Steps>
  <Step number={1} title="Find a trader">
    Browse the Traders page or live trade feed to find traders with strong track records.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Follow them">
    Click Follow and configure your copy trade settings (share size, limits).
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Trades execute automatically">
    When the trader enters a position, the same trade fires in your account in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

- **Follow multiple traders** simultaneously to diversify your strategy
- **Real-time notifications** when copy trades execute
- **Performance tracking** so you can evaluate which traders are worth following
- **Full control** -- adjust settings or unfollow at any time

<Callout type="tip">
  Start by following one or two traders with high win rates. Monitor their performance for a few days before adding more.
</Callout>

Copy trading works across all supported prediction markets. Every copied position is clearly attributed to the original trader so you always know where a trade came from.
