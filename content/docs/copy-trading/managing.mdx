# Managing Copy Trades

Once you are following traders, you have full control over your copy trade settings and can monitor performance from one place.

## Viewing your followed traders

Head to your **Profile page** to see all traders you currently follow. Each entry shows the trader's current stats and your copy trade configuration.

## Editing copy trade rules

Click the **pencil icon** next to any followed trader to adjust your settings. You can change share sizes, limits, and other parameters on a per-trader basis without unfollowing.

## Stopping copy trades

Click **Unfollow** to immediately stop copying a trader. No further trades from that trader will execute in your account.

## Notifications

- **Toast notifications** appear in real time when a copy trade executes
- **Notification bell** keeps a history of all copy trade activity so you can review later

<Callout type="tip">
  Check your notifications regularly to stay on top of what your followed traders are doing.
</Callout>

## Tracking performance

Every copied position shows which trader it came from with an **@ attribution** tag. This makes it easy to:

- See which traders are contributing to your profits (or losses)
- Compare copy trade performance against your own manual trades
- Decide which traders to keep following and which to drop
