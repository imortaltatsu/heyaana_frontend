# Following Traders

Finding the right traders to follow is the most important part of copy trading. HeyAana gives you the data you need to make informed decisions.

## Where to find traders

- **Traders page** (`/dashboard/traders`) -- browse and search all traders with detailed stats
- **Live trade feed** (`/dashboard/social`) -- spot active traders making moves in real time and follow them directly from the feed

## Evaluating a trader

Before you follow anyone, check their stats:

- **Win rate** -- percentage of profitable trades
- **ROI** -- return on investment over time
- **Profit** -- total dollar profit
- **Volume** -- how actively they trade
- **Alpha score** -- a composite score reflecting overall trading skill

<Callout type="info">
  A high win rate with low volume may mean a trader is selective. High volume with moderate win rate may indicate a more active strategy. Consider what fits your style.
</Callout>

## How to follow

1. Find a trader you like on either page
2. Click the **Follow** button on their profile
3. A confirmation modal appears where you can configure your copy trade settings (share size and limits)
4. Confirm to start copying

<Callout type="warning">
  Following a trader means real trades will execute in your account. Make sure you have sufficient balance before starting.
</Callout>
