# Watchlist

Keep tabs on the markets that matter to you without scrolling through everything.

## Adding Markets

Click the **star icon** on any market to add it to your watchlist. It's that simple. You'll see the star fill in to confirm it's saved.

## Viewing Your Watchlist

All your saved markets live at **/dashboard/watchlist**. Each entry shows the market's title and image so you can identify them at a glance.

## Quick Actions

From your watchlist, you can:

- **Click a market** to jump to its full details page
- **Click the trash icon** to remove a market you're no longer tracking

<Callout type="info">
Your watchlist is stored locally on your device and persists across sessions. No account sync needed -- your picks are always there when you come back.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Use your watchlist to build a shortlist of markets you're considering before committing to a trade. It's a handy way to monitor price movements without placing a position.
</Callout>
