# Welcome to HeyAnna

HeyAnna is the AI-powered trading terminal for prediction markets and an official Polymarket builder partner. Whether you are tracking whale moves, copy trading top performers, or letting AI signals handle your trades, HeyAnna gives you a serious edge.

## What you can do

- **Real-time market signals** -- AI-generated alerts on market movements as they happen
- **Copy trading** -- Follow top traders and mirror their positions automatically
- **Auto-trading** -- Let AI signals place trades for you based on configurable strategies
- **Whale and insider detection** -- See what the smart money is doing before the crowd
- **Portfolio analytics** -- Track your PnL, win rate, and performance over time

All trades execute on the Polygon network through Polymarket's order book.
