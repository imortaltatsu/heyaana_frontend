# Whale & Insider Alerts

Stay ahead of the market with real-time alerts when large or suspicious trades happen. HeyAana continuously monitors for whale movements and insider activity so you do not miss major signals.

## Where to find alerts

The **Auto Trade** page (`/dashboard/auto-trade`) shows a live feed of whale and insider movements. Alerts appear in real time as they are detected.

## What each alert includes

- **Market title** -- which prediction market the activity occurred in
- **Wallet address** -- the wallet that made the trade
- **Trade type** -- classified as either **whale** or **insider**
- **USD amount** -- the dollar value of the trade
- **Timestamp** -- exactly when the trade happened

<Callout type="info">
  Whale alerts track positions that exceed significant dollar thresholds. Insider alerts flag wallets with patterns suggesting informed trading.
</Callout>

## How to use these signals

Whale and insider alerts are informational -- they do not trigger automatic trades. Use them to:

- Spot markets attracting large money before the crowd notices
- Identify wallets that consistently make profitable moves
- Validate or challenge your own thesis on a market

<Callout type="tip">
  Click on any alert to open the trader's profile and see their full trading history.
</Callout>
